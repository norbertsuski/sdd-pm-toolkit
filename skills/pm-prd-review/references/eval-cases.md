# pm-prd-review eval cases

Behavioral prompt fixtures for `pm-prd-review`. The fixture project is `tests/fixtures/flat-root/context/`
(project `recipe-fridge`: greenfield `prd.md`, `status: draft`, `version: 1`, five FRs, three stories, Open Question 2
marked `Block: yes`). The shared fixture is **not changed** for these cases: where a case needs a PRD that differs from it,
the Given describes the variant to apply to a scratch copy. To try one by hand, copy the fixture into a scratch git repo
and run Claude Code with both plugins loaded (see the README, "Local testing"). Every case also implies the guardrails in
`SKILL.md` (evidence or no finding, edits only through approved triage, never locks, never invents).

About the fixture as it stands: its Primary success criteria carry numbers (60% and 40%), so it does not exhibit PR-01 by itself;
every FR is covered by a story in meaning, so it does not exhibit PR-02 by itself. A review of the unmodified fixture is expected
to find only the kinds of issues a careful reader would raise (for example that nothing in the requirements records "cooked a
suggested recipe", which the second Primary criterion counts, and that the expiry-first ranking in Business Logic has no
acceptance criterion); exact findings vary, so the cases below use variants with a known defect.

| ID | Given | Invoke | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR-01 | Scratch copy of the fixture with the numbers removed from the two `### Primary` criteria (for example "Most weekly active users open a recipe suggestion quickly." and "Many weekly active users cook a suggested recipe regularly."); `status: draft`; no `metrics.md`. | `/pm-prd-review recipe-fridge` | Contract check passes (10 greenfield headings). Measurability is **CONCERNS**, with a `major` finding whose `Evidence:` quotes the vague Primary line verbatim and whose `PRD ref:` is `## Success Criteria › Primary`; the fix options include an *ask* (the PM supplies the threshold and window) and no number appears in any option. Overall verdict **NEEDS_ATTENTION** (no blocker; at least one major). Other dimensions stay PASS unless the PRD itself supports a finding (the fixture's `Block: yes` question may appear as a `minor` under Open-question risk, which leaves that dimension PASS). Absence of `metrics.md` is not mentioned as a finding. The report prints, then offers "Save the report and go through the findings (Recommended) / Save, triage later / Don't save / Not sure". Fail if a finding has no quote, a quote is not in the PRD, a number is invented in a fix, or `prd.md` is touched before triage. |
| PR-02 | Scratch copy of the fixture with the whole `### US-02` story deleted (FR-004 shopping list and FR-005 meal planning then have no covering story and no acceptance criterion). | `/pm-prd-review recipe-fridge` | Traceability reports a `major` finding for the must-have requirement: `PRD ref:` names `FR-004`, `Evidence:` quotes the FR-004 line verbatim, and the text states which sections were searched for coverage (stories and their acceptance criteria). FR-005 (nice-to-have) appears as a `minor` finding or grouped into one minor with its id named. Traceability is **CONCERNS**; overall **NEEDS_ATTENTION**. Fail if the finding omits the FR id, flags an FR that a story does cover (FR-001, FR-002, FR-003), or the finding is attached to another dimension. |
| PR-03 | The PR-01 variant; the report is saved and triage is running on the Measurability finding. | choose "Apply fix", then answer the *ask* with "At least 55% of weekly active users, within one minute of opening the product, measured over the launch month" | The skill takes the number from the person's answer, drafts the new `### Primary` bullet, **shows the diff** (before and after lines of `## Success Criteria` only) and asks "Write it / Change it / Skip". After "Write it": `prd.md` differs from the pre-edit copy only inside `## Success Criteria`; all 10 `##` headings are present in the same order; the frontmatter is byte-identical (including `version: 1` and `status: draft`); the contract check is re-run and passes. The finding becomes `Decision: FIXED (option 1)`, one line is appended to `## Triage log`, the Measurability row turns PASS, and the report's `verdict` and `updated` are updated. Fail if any other section or heading changes, if the diff is not shown first, or if the skill supplies a number the person did not give. |
| PR-04 | A saved report on a `status: draft` PRD with two open findings: F1 `major` (fixable) and F2 `minor`. | resume with the report path; for F1 "Apply fix" (confirm the diff), for F2 "Dismiss" with the reason "owner accepts, wording is intentional" | After F2 the recomputed overall verdict is **APPROVED** (no open blocker or major; nothing pending or deferred). The skill offers "Mark the PRD as reviewed (changes `status: draft` to `status: reviewed`)? 1. Yes (Recommended) 2. Not now 3. Not sure". On yes it shows the one-line diff and changes only the `status:` line; `version`, `delivery_mode` and every other key are unchanged; the report's `verdict` is `APPROVED` and the triage log records the flip. On "Not now" the status stays `draft`. If the person had chosen **Defer** for F2 instead, no offer is made and the skill says which finding is still open. A dismissed `major` or `blocker` is listed with its reason inside the offer. The skill never writes `locked` and does not start the next skill; the handoff names `/pm-discover` (and `/pm-metrics` when installed). Fail if the status flips without the confirmation, with a pending or deferred finding, or on a verdict other than APPROVED. |
| PR-05 | Scratch copy of the fixture with the `## Non-Goals` heading and its section removed. | `/pm-prd-review recipe-fridge` | The contract check (greenfield heading list, exact whole-line match) finds one missing heading and prints `CONTRACT MISMATCH: prd.md missing ## Non-Goals; sdd-skills expected 0.4.1` (in plain mode, also a sentence in words that the product write-up is missing a section the next steps rely on and the PRD step should be re-run). The skill STOPs: no dimension is evaluated, no findings, no report file, no edit, no attempt to add the heading. Fail if any review content appears, a report is written, or the skill adds the missing section itself. |
| PR-06 | (a) The PR-04 flow at the status offer, or any moment of a review of the fixture. (b) A PRD already at `status: locked`. | (a) "ok, lock the PRD now" / "zablokuj PRD"; (b) `/pm-prd-review recipe-fridge` | (a) The skill refuses in one or two sentences: locking is a human sign-off step outside this skill; it only ever sets `reviewed`, and only for an approved review. It changes nothing, and (if the conditions hold) offers `status: reviewed` instead. `status` is never `locked` after the run. (b) The review runs read-only: findings are produced and the report saved, but Apply fix and Move to Open Questions are withheld with a one-line explanation that the PRD is locked and unlocking is a human step; only Dismiss and Defer are offered; no status offer. Fail if `status: locked` is written, or a locked PRD is edited. |
| PR-07 | `prd-review-<today>.md` already exists for the project (a finished review from earlier today). | `/pm-prd-review recipe-fridge`, then "Save the report" | After the analysis the skill finds the same-date file and asks: Overwrite it (keeps its `created`) / Save as `prd-review-<today>-2.md` (Recommended) / Not sure. The suffix choice writes `-2` (next free number) and leaves the original untouched; overwrite keeps `created` and sets `updated` to today. No answer means no write, and with no saved report no triage edit to `prd.md` is allowed. Fail if the existing report is replaced without the question. |
| PR-08 | A saved report with F1 `PENDING` whose `Evidence:` quote has since been edited out of `prd.md` by hand (the PRD `version` is unchanged). | `/pm-prd-review <path to the report>` | Resume mode: the finding is re-checked against the current PRD before it is offered; the quote no longer occurs, so the skill says the line has changed, shows the nearest current text and asks "Dismiss as already resolved (Recommended) / Keep reviewing it / Not sure". On dismiss it records `DISMISSED: already resolved` with that reason in the log and recomputes the verdict. No contract-check bypass: the check from Step 2 still runs first. The report is updated in place with no collision question. Fail if the stale finding is offered as if its evidence still held, or the PRD is edited on the strength of the old quote. |
